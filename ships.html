Flagship: Arquitens Light Cruiser “Duranium Gambit”

The Duranium Gambit serves as Rivok Scal’s flagship — a compact Arquitens‑class cruiser with a reputation for punching above its tonnage. Rivok won the vessel in a high‑stakes game of chance on Nar Shaddaa, wagering a pod‑race sponsorship against a droid pit‑fight syndicate. When the dust settled, the Gambit was his prize — battered, half‑functional, and perfect for refitting.

Now restored and reinforced with scavenged plating and slicer‑grade systems, the cruiser operates as the fleet’s command ship. Its hull bears the marks of countless salvage runs, but its engines hum with quiet menace. Few realize how much firepower hides behind its “civilian” registry.

Foray Class Blockade Runner “Nexu's Grin”

Fast, sleek, and deceptively armed, the Nexu's Grin was captured by Rivok himself during a salvage operation gone sideways. When a rival crew tried to claim the wreck, Rivok deployed his droid soldiers, stormed the bridge, and commandeered the vessel before its reactors cooled.

The ship’s narrow profile and over‑tuned engines make it ideal for rapid insertion and extraction missions. Officially, it’s listed as a “salvage scout,” but its sensor suite and reinforced hull tell a different story — one of precision raids and quiet disappearances.

Salvage class Corvette "Patchwork Golem"

The Patchwork Golem began life as a derelict patrol corvette, recovered from a forgotten scrapyard orbiting Raxus Prime. Rivok’s crew restored it piece by piece, replacing its gutted systems with modular salvage gear and defensive turrets.

Now repurposed as a patrol ship near Rivok’s holdings, the Golem guards hyperspace lanes and coordinates recovery operations. Its scanners can pick up faint ion trails and debris signatures across entire sectors, making it invaluable for both legitimate salvage and less‑official pursuits.

Its cavernous bay houses Rivok’s luxury sail barge, a mobile command lounge disguised as a pleasure craft.
