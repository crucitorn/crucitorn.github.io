Pledged Personnel — Scal Group Holdings

C2-R4 “Cutwire” — Slicer Support Droid

C2-R4, nicknamed Cutwire, is Rivok’s primary slicer‑assistant droid. Originally a battlefield infiltration unit, Cutwire was recovered from a derelict Separatist depot and rebuilt with modern firmware. The droid is pledged to Rivok through a long‑standing service contract and handles subsystem breaches, encrypted data extraction, and on‑site terminal manipulation. The little droid follows the Hutt everywhere.

BAF-1000 Droids — The Triplets

A small trio of BAF-1000 were the first entities to become servants of the Hutt.

“The Ebon Guard” — FN-D0 Assassin Droid

The Ebon Guard is a battallion of refurbished combat droids pledged to Rivok after he liberated them from a pirate warlord’s control matrix. Their plating is mismatched and and tarnished, giving them their name. They serve as Rivok’s boarding team, security detail, and rapid‑response force during salvage operations that turn hostile. The droids are programmed for loyalty and respond to Rivok's command alone.

Alruia Clan — Lasat Enforcers

The Alruia Clan is one of the few organic worker groups pledged to Rivok. A Lasat clan with a long history of mercenary work, the Alruia Clan entered Rivok’s service after the Hutt saved their leader from a failed bounty contract. Bound by honor and debt, Aayla Alruia, the clan's matron, acts as Rivok’s field enforcer, negotiator, and muscle when diplomacy fails.

Happy Blasters

The Happy Blasters are a mercenary unit consigned to Rivok. They are a Gran marksman unit, highly trained and accurate. Rivok pays them handsomely for their loyalty.
