Origins

Rivok Scal was born far from the influence of the great kajidics, raised among spacer clans who valued independence over lineage. Unlike most Hutts, Rivok grew up in hangars, scrapyards, and makeshift workshops scattered across the Outer Rim. His early life was defined by starship parts, half‑functional droids, and the constant hum of engines. He learned to fly before he learned to speak Huttese properly, and he learned to slice before he learned to trust anyone.
