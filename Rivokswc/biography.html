PERSONNEL DOSSIER: RIVOK SCAL

Independent Hutt Spacer — Verified Identity Record

Origins

Rivok Scal was born far from the influence of the great kajidics, raised among spacer clans who valued independence over lineage. Unlike most Hutts, Rivok grew up in hangars, scrapyards, and makeshift workshops scattered across the Outer Rim. His early life was defined by starship parts, half‑functional droids, and the constant hum of engines. He learned to fly before he learned to speak Huttese properly, and he learned to slice before he learned to trust anyone.

Rise to Independence

Rivok’s reputation began with small salvage contracts — legitimate work that kept him fed and mobile. But his skill with outdated systems, corrupted data cores, and forgotten starship firmware quickly earned him a different kind of clientele. He became the spacer you called when you needed a lock bypassed, a ship quietly moved, or a problem solved without paperwork. Rivok never advertised; his work spoke for itself.

His independence was hard‑won. Rivok refused kajidic sponsorships, refused cartel protection, and refused to be anyone’s asset. Every credit he earned went into building a fleet that could keep him mobile, safe, and untraceable.

The Salvage Fleet

The Expeditionary Salvage Fleet began as a single freighter and a handful of loyal droids. Over time, Rivok expanded his operations, acquiring ships through auctions, reclamation contracts, and — occasionally — more direct methods. The fleet’s official purpose is salvage and reclamation, but its mobility and structure make it ideal for Rivok’s quieter dealings: discreet transport, sensitive data recovery, and bounty operations that require subtlety.

Every ship in the fleet carries Rivok’s personal modifications: encrypted comms, slicer‑grade avionics, and hidden compartments that only he and his pledged workers know how to access.

Current Status

Today, Rivok Scal commands a respected salvage fleet with a shadow reputation. Spacers know him as reliable, calm, and surprisingly approachable for a Hutt. His enemies know him as a problem that doesn’t escalate unless forced — and one that never forgets. Rivok travels the galaxy with purpose, carving out a future built on independence, loyalty, and quiet efficiency.

Where Rivok goes, opportunity follows. And where opportunity fails, Rivok adapts — as he always has.
