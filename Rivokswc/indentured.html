INDENTURED WORKER REGISTRY

Pledged Personnel — Scal Group Holdings

BX‑77 “Cutwire” — Slicer Support Droid

BX‑77, nicknamed Cutwire, is Rivok’s primary slicer‑assistant droid. Originally a battlefield infiltration unit, Cutwire was recovered from a derelict Separatist depot and rebuilt with modern firmware. The droid is pledged to Rivok through a long‑standing service contract and handles subsystem breaches, encrypted data extraction, and on‑site terminal manipulation.

R‑4 Mechanic Droids — Salvage Crew

A small cadre of R‑4 units forms the backbone of Rivok’s salvage operations. These droids were acquired piecemeal from scrapyards, auctions, and abandoned mining outposts. Each one has been reprogrammed to serve the Scal Group indefinitely, maintaining ships, repairing hull damage, and performing hazardous recovery tasks that organic crew members prefer to avoid.

“The Bronze Guard” — Droid Soldiers

The Bronze Guard is a squad of refurbished combat droids pledged to Rivok after he liberated them from a pirate warlord’s control matrix. Their plating is mismatched and oxidized, giving them their name. They serve as Rivok’s boarding team, security detail, and rapid‑response force during salvage operations that turn hostile.

Gorvath — Nikto Enforcer

Gorvath is one of the few organic workers pledged to Rivok. A Nikto warrior with a long history of mercenary work, Gorvath entered Rivok’s service after the Hutt saved him from a failed bounty contract. Bound by honor and debt, Gorvath acts as Rivok’s field enforcer, negotiator, and muscle when diplomacy fails.

TK‑Luma — Logistics Droid

TK‑Luma oversees inventory, cargo manifests, and fleet logistics. The droid was originally designed for corporate shipping but was abandoned after its company collapsed. Rivok acquired Luma during a salvage auction and pledged it to long‑term service. Luma’s efficiency keeps the fleet supplied, organized, and ready for rapid deployment.
